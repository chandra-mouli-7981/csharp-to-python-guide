System Design · What happens if…?
How to structure any "what happens if…" answer
  1. Clarify: scale, consistency needs, what "failure" means (crash, slow, partition, duplicate).
  2. Trace the failure: walk the timeline step by step. What state is each component in?
  3. Impact: who is affected (data loss, duplicates, downtime, wrong results)? How bad?
  4. Detect: which metric, log or alert tells you?
  5. Mitigate and fix: immediate action, then the design change (idempotency, locking, backoff, fencing...).
  6. Trade-offs: what does your fix cost (latency, complexity, availability)? Name the alternatives.
